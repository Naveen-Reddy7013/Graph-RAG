Tony Stark stands on the high balcony of Stark Tower, 
looking out over the glowing skyline of New York City. 
Stark Tower is his absolute pride and joy, 
a modern monument to clean energy. 
Inside the main laboratory, 
Pepper Potts is reviewing the flight logs for the Stark Industries jet. 
Tony Stark enters and speaks with Pepper Potts about their upcoming research trip to Malibu. 
Pepper Potts reminds him that the core arc reactor inside Stark Tower requires urgent maintenance, 
warning him of potential system failures if it is neglected.
