Meanwhile, in the dim shadows of New York City, Obadiah Stane plans his hostile corporate takeover. Obadiah Stane is deeply jealous of Tony Stark and covets the technology in Stark Tower. Under the cover of night, Obadiah Stane sneaks into Stark Tower to bypass the security systems. In the lobby, Obadiah Stane confronts Pepper Potts, who is carrying an encrypted USB drive containing vital corporate secrets. Obadiah Stane threatens Pepper Potts, demanding the files, but Tony Stark suddenly arrives, confronting Obadiah Stane and forcing him to leave the premises.
